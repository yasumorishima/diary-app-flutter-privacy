# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 28, 2026**

Daily Diary (hereinafter referred to as "the App") respects the privacy of our users and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
The App stores the following information locally on your device:
- Diary content (text)
- Diary creation date and time
- Mood records
- Tag information

**This information is stored only on your device and is NOT sent to external servers.**
(Only if you enable the optional Google Drive backup feature, your data is also stored in your own Google Drive. See Section 4, "Google Sign-In and Google Drive Backup (Optional)".)

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
Since version 1.0.44 the App shows no ads and contains no ad SDK (Google AdMob). It does not automatically collect an advertising ID, device information or usage data.

### 1.3 Age Signals (Google Play)
For users in regions where the law requires app stores to share age information (such as California in the United States and Brazil), the App requests an age signal (an age range such as "13-15") from Google Play through the Play Age Signals API when it starts. Whether an age range is shared is decided by your Google Play settings (or, for a supervised account, by the parent).

- The App has no ads and no features that change with age, so the age range it receives is not used.
- The age range is not stored, and it is never sent to the developer's servers or to third parties.
- Users outside those regions are not asked to share their age.

## 2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used for the following purposes:
- Providing diary functionality (local storage only)
- Improving the App

## 3. Data Storage

- **Diary Data**: Stored only in local storage on your device. The developer and third parties cannot access this data.
- **Google Drive Backup (Optional)**: If enabled, your diary data is also stored in a dedicated app area (appDataFolder) inside your own Google Drive. Only this App can access that area; the developer cannot access it at all.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

### Google Sign-In and Google Drive Backup (Optional)
The App offers an optional Google Drive backup feature. When you enable it:

- The App uses Google Sign-In. It accesses only your basic account information (such as your email address, for display) and a dedicated app area in your Google Drive (appDataFolder; scope: drive.appdata).
- Your diary backup is stored **only in the dedicated app area of your own Google Drive**. It is never sent to the developer’s servers, and the developer cannot read your backup.
- If you do not use this feature, no Google account is required and your diary data never leaves your device.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Data Deletion

### 5.1 Deleting Data Within the App
You can delete individual diary entries within the App. Deleted diary data is permanently removed from your device and cannot be recovered.

### 5.2 Deleting All App Data
You can delete all data from the App using either of the following methods:
- **Uninstalling the App**: Uninstalling the App will delete all diary data stored on your device.
- **Clearing App Storage**: Go to Android Settings > Apps > Daily Diary > Storage > Clear Data to delete all app data.

### 5.3 Data Export
The App includes a feature to export diary data. If you wish to keep your data, please export it before deleting.

### 5.4 Deleting Your Google Drive Backup
If you use Google Drive backup, you can delete the backup file from your Drive at any time via Settings > Google Drive Backup > Delete backup in the App. You can also disconnect the App and delete its data from Google Drive settings (Settings > Manage apps).

### 5.5 Advertising Data from Earlier Versions
Versions 1.0.43 and earlier showed ads through Google AdMob. Data that AdMob collected then is handled and deleted under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Security

The App takes reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or data storage is 100% secure.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as necessary. When changes are made, we will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last Updated" date.

## 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please send us a review or feedback through the Google Play Store app page.
